## Qualification Criteria

Per Brett's requirements, coins must demonstrate:
- **NOT a meme coin** — no pure speculation/hype tokens
- **Survived ≥1 full crypto cycle** (bull + bear, e.g., 2017–18, 2020–22)
- **Real utility** — genuine market dynamics, not just hype
- **Deep liquidity** — listed on 5+ major exchanges
- **Institutional/reputable backing** — VC, enterprise, or strong dev teams
- **Large market cap** — ideally >$1B, strong preference for >$10B
- **Mature, consistent** — predictable signals for phase-riding

## Scoring Methodology

**Qualification Score (1–10)** weighted composite:
| Factor | Weight | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Not Meme | 20% | 0 if pure meme, 10 if zero meme risk |
| Cycles Survived | 20% | 0=0, 1=5, 2+=10 |
| Real Utility | 15% | Genuine use case and adoption |
| Liquidity Depth | 15% | Exchange coverage and volume |
| Institutional Backing | 15% | VC, enterprise, ETF presence |
| Market Cap | 15% | Mega=10, Large=8, Mid=5, Small=2 |

**Recommendation thresholds:** QUALIFIED ≥ 7 | BORDERLINE 5–6 | EXCLUDE < 5
