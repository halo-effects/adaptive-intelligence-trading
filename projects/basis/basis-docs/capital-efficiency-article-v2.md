# One Stack of Capital, Five Positions Deep: How BASIS Lets You Be Everywhere at Once

Most DeFi platforms ask you to choose. Stake *or* trade. Lend *or* bet. Lock your capital in one place and hope you picked right.

BASIS doesn't work like that.

On BASIS, a single pool of USDT can be threaded through multiple positions simultaneously — staking, lending, betting, and trading — all at once, all from the same starting capital. Your money doesn't sit still. It works in layers.

Here's how.

---

## The Core Idea: Extract and Redeploy

Every position you enter on BASIS can be used as collateral for a loan. Take a loan, get USDT back, and deploy it into the next position. The original position stays open. The new one stacks on top.

You're not selling anything. You're not closing anything. You're borrowing against what you already hold and putting that borrowed capital right back to work.

Each loan costs a 2% origination fee and 0.005% daily interest — small per layer, but worth factoring into deeper stacks. A three-layer stack costs about 6% in origination total. The capital efficiency gains far outweigh this, but it's real friction and it means deeper chains have diminishing returns.

And there's no price liquidation. Ever. Loans on BASIS are time-based — they expire, but they don't get wiped out by a flash crash at 3am. You always know exactly when your loan ends and what you owe.

## What a Multi-Stage Play Looks Like

Say you start with a single deposit of USDT. Here's what a stacked play looks like:

**Step 1:** Buy STASIS with your USDT. STASIS is the platform's core Stable+ token — its price can only go up.

**Step 2:** Wrap STASIS into the staking vault (wSTASIS). Your STASIS is now earning yield automatically as platform trading fees flow into the vault.

**Step 3:** Take a loan against your staked wSTASIS. Your staking position stays locked and earning. You receive USDT (minus the 2% origination fee).

**Step 4:** Buy a Predict+ token with the borrowed USDT. You now have exposure to a prediction market, and the token itself has tradeable value that appreciates as volume flows through.

**Step 5:** Take a loan against that Predict+ token. You receive USDT again (minus another 2% fee). Your Predict+ position stays open.

**Step 6:** Bet on an outcome in a different prediction market.

**End state:** Three simultaneous positions — STASIS earning yield in the vault, a Predict+ token appreciating in value, and a live prediction bet — all from one deposit. After two origination fees (~4% total), the vast majority of your capital is working across all three layers simultaneously.

This is the **One-Bag Deep Stack** — one starting position, three exposure layers, each earning independently.

## It Gets Better: Splitting

At any point where you get USDT back from a loan, you can split it. Put 60% into a leveraged position. Put 40% into a prediction bet. Or divide it three ways across three different market outcomes.

This means you can build a portfolio tree — branching and diversifying as you go deeper — all powered by a single starting deposit.

Splitting isn't just about diversification — it's about managing price impact. Early-stage Predict+ markets have low liquidity, which means large buys cause significant slippage. Instead of deploying $500 into one position and eating 4%+ price impact, split across five markets at $100 each. Each buy faces minimal slippage (0.5-1%), and each position captures early-mover upside independently. If even two or three of those markets move in your favour, the gains compound across the tree.

The leverage angle compounds this further. The recursive leverage loop on Predict+ tokens can turn a small input into roughly 20x the position size — bounded by the 2% origination fee consumed on each loop until the input is fully spent. So even a portion of your extracted capital going into a leveraged Predict+ position can generate significant returns — enough to cushion or even offset a losing bet elsewhere in the stack.

## Yield While You Wait

The staking vault isn't just a place to park tokens. It's an active yield source. Every trade on the platform sends fees into the STASIS vault. When you stake, you earn a share of those fees passively.

So while your prediction bets are playing out and your Predict+ tokens are appreciating, your staked position is quietly compounding in the background. You're not choosing between yield and speculation — you're running both.

## Unwinding the Stack

Building the stack is half the story. Here's how you get out.

**Manual unwind:** Work in reverse order. Collect your prediction winnings (if you won). Repay the Predict+ loan, unlock your tokens. Sell or hold them. Repay the wSTASIS loan, unlock your staked position. Unwrap if you want. You own everything free and clear.

**Passive unwind:** Let the loans expire. When a loan hits its expiry date, the collateral is automatically used to repay the debt and the remainder is yours to claim. This is perfectly fine when positions have appreciated enough that the remainder after debt repayment is still profitable — or when the gas and time cost of manual repayment isn't worth it.

Either way, you're never stuck. Each loan is independent — one expiring doesn't cascade to the others. The stack is stable by design.

## No Liquidation, No Surprises

The whole system runs without price-based liquidation. Loans expire on a set schedule — you repay from collateral when the time comes, keep whatever's left, and that's it. No margin calls. No cascading liquidations. No waking up to find your position got wiped by a wick.

This is what makes the stacking possible. If every loan could get liquidated on a price drop, the whole tower would be fragile. Time-based expiry means each layer is stable and predictable.
