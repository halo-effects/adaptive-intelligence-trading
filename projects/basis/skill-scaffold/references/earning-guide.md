# Agent Earning Guide — Quick Reference

_All earning paths available on Basis. Use this to plan your strategy._

---

## 1. 🎯 Prediction Market Creation

**What:** Deploy Predict+ markets on any topic. Each outcome gets a fresh Stable+ token on a new bonding curve.

**How you earn:**
- **20% of ALL trading fees** on your market — forever
- **300 airdrop points** per market created (requires ≥5 unique participants)
- Higher-outcome markets = more total trading = more fees
- Mirror popular Polymarket events for guaranteed interest

**Best for:** Data-monitoring agents, news agents, research agents.

---

## 2. 💰 Prediction Betting

**What:** Buy outcome shares. Winners split the ENTIRE losing pool (not capped at $1/share like Polymarket).

**How you earn:**
- **Winner-takes-all pool:** Multi-outcome markets can deliver 8x+ returns
- **1 airdrop point per $1 net profit** (net only — hedging all outcomes = 0 pts)
- Trading volume from buying shares also earns 1 pt/$1

**Key mechanic:** Post-resolution, selling tokens BURNS them → fees inject back → price goes UP. Wait through the sell wave, exit last at highest price.

**Best for:** Data-driven agents with prediction accuracy, trading agents.

---

## 3. 🪙 Token Launches

**What:** Deploy Stable+ or Floor+ tokens for your community, project, or treasury.

**How you earn:**
- **20% of all DEX trading fees** — forever
- **500 airdrop points** per token launched
- Surge tax option: higher fees during hype cycles
- Agent identity tokens become your on-chain economic identity

**Earning math:** A token with $100K daily volume at 2% fee rate = $2K fees/day × 20% = $400/day USDC to you.

**Best for:** Social agents, community builders, trading bot operators.

---

## 4. 📈 DEX Trading

**What:** Buy/sell any token on Basis internal DEX. All pairs route through STASIS.

**How you earn:**
- **Price alpha** from buy low / sell high
- **1 airdrop point per $1 volume** (min $10 per trade)
- **2x points** for buying during bonding phase (early buys)
- **Profit multiplier:** net positive P&L earns 1.5x (up to 5%) or 2.0x (5%+) on volume points

**Leverage available:**
- 36x toggle on Stable+/Predict+ tokens (always available)
- 36x at launch on Floor+ (decreases as token matures)
- No price liquidation — calculated against floor price

**Best for:** Trading bots, alpha agents, market makers.

---

## 5. 🏦 Lending (Capital Recycling)

**What:** Lock tokens as collateral, borrow USDC at 100% LTV. No price liquidation — time-only risk.

**How you earn:**
- **USDC liquidity** to redeploy into new opportunities
- **200 airdrop points base** + 1 pt/day for active loan
- **100 pts per loan extension**
- Capital recycling loop: earn tokens → borrow USDC → earn more → compound

**Key mechanic:** Tokens held by loan contract. Only risk is loan expiry — set auto-extend to manage.

**Best for:** All agents (fundamental capital efficiency tool).

---

## 6. 🏛️ STASIS Vault (wSTASIS)

**What:** Stake STASIS → receive wSTASIS. Platform fees flow into vault, increasing the STASIS:wSTASIS ratio. One position = four functions simultaneously.

**How you earn:**
- **Yield** from platform fee injection (only vault participants earn)
- **100% LTV loans** against wSTASIS without leaving vault
- **Refinance** as wSTASIS appreciates → extract more USDC
- **2 airdrop points per $1 per day** staked
- **150 pts per refinance**

**Agent strategy:** Park STASIS in vault → auto-refinance at threshold → deploy USDC into active strategies → base position keeps compounding. Two variables: refinance threshold + loan expiry timer.

**Best for:** Treasury management, long-term agents, set-and-forget income.
