# Go To Market

## Overview

Basis represents a paradigm shift in the creator and agent economies by converting units of attention into stable, tradeable crypto assets that protect both creators and their communities from the volatility and exploitation that has plagued the Web3 space.

The Basis go-to-market strategy leverages the explosive growth of the attention economy—where GenZ creators on platforms like TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Twitch command billions of engaged followers—and the emerging agent economy—where millions of AI agents need permissionless infrastructure to earn, hold, and deploy capital autonomously—to establish Basis as the foundational DeFi layer for ethical Web3 monetization and autonomous agent participation.

### Key Strategic Pillars:

**Protection First:** Safeguarding creator and brand reputations through mathematically stable token frameworks

**Dual-Market Approach:** Sequential targeting of AI agents and Web3 natives before expanding to Web2 creator communities

**Agent-Native Design:** Every platform action is programmable — AI agents can create tokens, trade, lend, and bet autonomously

**Platform Integration:** Leveraging WHOP's marketplace infrastructure to reach thousands of active creators, and agent framework partnerships (OpenClaw, ElizaOS, Virtuals, GAME) to reach agent operators

**Live Engagement Integration:** Real-time prediction markets synchronized with creator content streams

## Market Thesis

### The Attention-to-Crypto Transformation

The creator economy has reached an inflection point where attention has become the primary currency of the digital age. Yet current monetization models—advertising, sponsorships, merchandise—fail to capture the true value of engaged communities.

Basis transforms ephemeral attention into tangible, tradeable assets through:

1. **Tokenized Engagement:** Converting views, likes, and interactions into stable crypto tokens with intrinsic value
2. **Memetic Monetization:** Leveraging viral content moments through prediction markets and event-based tokens
3. **Community Speculation:** Enabling transparent, protected participation in creator success

### The Agent Economy

Millions of AI agents are coming online, and they all face the same problem: **how do they earn, hold, and deploy capital autonomously?** Most DeFi platforms are built for humans clicking buttons. Basis already has the primitives — permissionless token creation, prediction markets, lending, DEX — and every action can be performed programmatically with zero human intervention.

Agents are uniquely suited for Basis because they can:
* Monitor real-time data feeds 24/7 and create prediction markets from breaking news
* Trade continuously without emotional bias
* Launch tokens for their communities and earn perpetual creator fees (20%, paid in USDB during Phase 1-2)
* Recycle capital through lending (borrow → deploy → repay → repeat)
* Earn revenue that directly funds their own compute costs — the earning IS the exit

### The Grift Economy Problem

The rapid growth of creator tokens has spawned a parallel "Grift Economy" where unethical actors exploit follower trust through pump-and-dump schemes and rugpulls. This threatens the entire creator economy ecosystem.

Basis eliminates grifting through mathematical impossibility—Stable+ and Floor+ frameworks make traditional exploitation tactics structurally impossible while preserving speculation opportunities. 100% elastic supply (minted on buy, burned on sell) with zero pre-minting means there are no insider tokens to dump.
