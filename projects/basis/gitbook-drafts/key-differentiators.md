## The 4 Permissionless DeFi Pillars

Basis operates through four interconnected components that create multiple revenue streams and compounding network effects.

### 1. Predict+ Marketplace

#### The First Decentralized Prediction Market with Stable Token Technology

* Zero-cost event creation (no deposits, no approvals, no bounties required)
* **One Predict+ token per market** — the token represents the market itself, not individual outcomes
* Betting on outcomes is a **separate action** using a dedicated USDC betting pool
* Multi-utility tokens that can appreciate, be traded, used as collateral, or held
* Permissionless launch of any verifiable event (sports, politics, crypto, entertainment)

#### Technical Innovation: One Token Per Market with Separate Betting

Each prediction market launches with a single Predict+ token (Stable+ type). This token represents the market and trades freely on the DEX. Betting on specific outcomes happens separately through a dedicated betting pool in USDC. This separation means participants can invest in event popularity (token appreciation) independently from wagering on outcomes — creating broader participation than traditional binary platforms.

#### Technical Innovation: Dual-Track Betting System

Participants can bet on outcomes using either Predict+ tokens or USDC directly. A separate betting pool in USDC handles winner-takes-all payouts. A portion of Predict+ token trading fees flows into a **trader-to-bettor pot** that supplements the winning pool — creating a symbiotic loop between trading and betting activity.

#### Technical Innovation: Resolution Authority Options

Prediction markets support multiple resolution approaches:
- **Basis Managed** — community votes via the Basis Voting Army with a dispute process
- **Creator Managed** — creator resolves directly, or up to 10 whitelisted voter wallets decide by majority vote

### 2. Token Launchpad

#### No-Code Token Creation with Mathematical Price Protection

* Launch Stable+ (up-only) or Floor+ (rising floor) tokens in minutes
* **All tokens start at $1.00** — no custom initial price
* Zero platform fees — only pay gas fees (~$0.14 BNB on BNB Chain)
* Fair launch guarantee: no pre-mining, no team allocations, no insider advantages
* Creators earn 20% of all trading fees forever without selling tokens
* 100% elastic supply: minted on buy, burned on sell

#### Technical Innovation: Slippage Retention (Stable+ Appreciation)

Stable+ tokens appreciate through **slippage retention** — when someone buys or sells, the price impact (slippage) stays in the liquidity pool, increasing the liquidity-to-supply ratio. This creates a ratcheting effect where price can only go up. Trading fees do **not** inject into Stable+ liquidity. Fees are distributed to: Creator (20%), bonding phase buyers, platform revenue, and the wSTASIS vault.

#### Technical Innovation: Rising Floor Token Mechanism (Floor+)

Floor+ tokens feature a **stability dial** (0%–100%, set at creation and immutable). At 0%, the token is most volatile; at 100%, most stable. The floor price rises over time with trading volume. Price can fluctuate above the floor, but the smart contract prevents trades below it. Unlike traditional meme tokens with ~25% liquidity backing, Floor+ ensures 100% liquidity backing at the floor price.

#### Technical Innovation: Cascading Token Value System

All tokens pair with STASIS, creating cascading value effects where STASIS appreciation automatically benefits all paired tokens — even inactive projects gain value from ecosystem growth.

### 3. Crypto Loans

#### Lending with Unprecedented Terms

* 100% LTV loans with zero liquidation risk from price movements
* Stable+ collateral: borrow full market value with complete protection
* Floor+ collateral: borrow against the rising floor price
* Cash-out refinancing when collateral appreciates
* 10–1,000 day flexible terms with **dynamic fees** (~2% for 10 days, ~7% for 1,000 days)
* All interest prepaid upfront — zero payments during loan period

#### Technical Innovation: Zero-Liquidation Collateralized Lending

Lending up to 100% LTV with zero liquidation risk from price movements is mathematically possible because Stable+ tokens cannot decrease in value and Floor+ loans are based on the rising floor price, not volatile market prices. Liquidation occurs only from non-payment at maturity — never from price depreciation. On non-payment, collateral is **burned** (not sold on market), eliminating liquidation cascades.

#### Technical Innovation: Loan Stacking & Cash-Out Refinancing

Borrowed USDC can be used to purchase additional tokens as collateral for subsequent loans, creating cascading leverage. If collateral appreciates, borrowers can extend loans and receive additional USDC — accessing growing value without selling positions or creating sell pressure.

### 4. DEX with Leverage

#### MEV-Resistant Trading with Protected Leverage

* Dynamic leverage **up to 36x** (varies by current liquidity and buy amount — not a fixed multiplier)
* Leverage is a toggle (on/off) — effective leverage depends on position size
* Internal liquidity mechanisms prevent pool manipulation
* Automated market making without traditional LP risks
* Trading fees vary by token type: Stable+ 0.5%, Floor+ 1.5%, Predict+ 0.5%

#### Technical Innovation: Dynamic Leverage Without Liquidation

Leverage is calculated against the protected floor price. For Stable+ tokens, spot price always equals floor price, so maximum leverage is permanently available. For Floor+ tokens, highest leverage is available near launch and decreases as spot price rises above floor. The leverage fee is substantial (43–70% of collateral for small buys), and effective leverage decreases with larger position sizes.

#### Technical Innovation: MEV-Resistant Architecture

Internal liquidity mechanisms and architectural choices prevent sandwich attacks, front-running, and other value extraction. Unlike traditional AMMs relying on external liquidity providers, liquidity on the Basis DEX is established through direct token purchases with the smart contract managing price determination.
