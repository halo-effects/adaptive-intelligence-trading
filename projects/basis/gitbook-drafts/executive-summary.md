# Executive Summary

## Basis — Permissionless DeFi Infrastructure

**Basis** is a permissionless DeFi layer that enables anyone — creators, communities, traders, and AI agents — to launch tokens, trade, lend, and predict outcomes using mathematically protected financial primitives. Every token on Basis has built-in price protection, every loan has zero liquidation risk from price movements, and every prediction market creates multi-utility assets that go far beyond binary betting.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Pure Yield Model:** 90% of all platform revenue flows directly to BASIS stakers as USDC. Token value derives from real yield, not speculation.
{% endhint %}

### The Four Pillars

Basis operates through four interconnected, permissionless components:

1. **Token Launchpad** — Create **Stable+** (up-only) or **Floor+** (rising floor) tokens in minutes with zero platform fees. Tokens are 100% elastic supply — minted on buy, burned on sell — with no pre-mining and no insider allocations.

2. **Predict+ Marketplace** — The first decentralized prediction market with stable token technology. One **Predict+** token per market represents the event itself. Betting on outcomes is a separate action using a dedicated USDC betting pool. Tokens can also be held for appreciation, traded on the DEX, or used as loan collateral.

3. **Crypto Loans** — Borrow up to 100% LTV against Basis tokens with zero liquidation risk from price movements. Loan terms range from 10 to 1,000 days with dynamic fees (approximately 2% for 10 days to 7% for 1,000 days), all prepaid upfront.

4. **DEX (Decentralized Exchange)** — MEV-resistant trading with dynamic leverage (up to 36x theoretical maximum, varying by liquidity and buy size). Internal liquidity mechanisms prevent pool manipulation. No external liquidity providers needed.

### How It Works

All Basis tokens pair against **STASIS**, the platform's native Stable+ liquidity token (which itself pairs with USDC). This creates a **cascading growth effect** — when STASIS appreciates through ecosystem activity, every token paired against it benefits.

**All tokens start at $1.00** with creator-configured starting liquidity ($100–$10,000). Supply is 100% elastic: minted when purchased, burned when sold. There are zero pre-minted tokens, zero team allocations, and zero insider advantages.

### Revenue Model

Trading fees vary by token type (platform-set, not creator-configurable):

| Token Type | Trading Fee |
| ---------- | ----------- |
| Stable+    | 0.5%        |
| Floor+     | 1.5%        |
| Predict+   | 0.5%        |

**Fee Waterfall:**
Trading Fee → Creator (20%) → Bonding phase buyers → STASIS Vault (portion) → Platform Revenue (remainder) → 90% to BASIS Vault stakers as USDC + 10% platform operations.

Creators earn 20% of all trading fees on their token in perpetuity — paid in USDC, not tokens. No selling required. Bonding phase participants earn a perpetual share of fees for early support. BASIS stakers receive 90% of net platform revenue as USDC yield.

### The Agent Economy

Basis is designed for both human and AI agent participation. Every action on the platform can be performed programmatically — token creation, trading, lending, prediction market creation, and betting. AI agents can:

- Create prediction markets from real-time data feeds
- Trade 24/7 with no human intervention
- Launch tokens for their communities
- Recycle capital through the lending facility
- Earn USDC revenue that directly funds their own compute costs

This makes Basis the permissionless DeFi layer where AI agents and humans earn, create, and grow together.

### Key Innovations

- **Slippage retention** drives Stable+ appreciation — price impact from trades stays in the liquidity pool, increasing the liquidity-to-supply ratio
- **100% LTV lending** with zero price-based liquidation — mathematically possible because Stable+ collateral cannot decrease in value
- **Dynamic leverage** up to 36x with no liquidation risk for Stable+ tokens
- **Multi-utility prediction tokens** that can be held, traded, collateralized, and bet with
- **Fair launch guarantee** — zero pre-minting, zero team allocations across all token types
- **MEV-resistant architecture** — internal liquidity prevents sandwich attacks and front-running
