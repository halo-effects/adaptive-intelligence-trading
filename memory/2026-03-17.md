### TP Catch-Up Bug Found & Fixed (Paper Bots Only)
- **Bug**: V14PM dashboard showed ZRO and PENDLE above their TP targets but not selling
- **Root cause**: Daily tick in `v14_lifecycle_engine.py` runs `_long_dca_tick` with PREVIOUS day's OHLC. If today's price gapped above TP but yesterday's high was below TP → engine adds DCA layers instead of selling → avg entry and TP recalculate upward → missed exit
- **Fix**: Added "Live TP catch-up" block after daily tick in `tick()` method. After daily tick completes, checks current candle price against TP. If exceeded, runs `_long_dca_tick` with current data. Safe: current price > avg entry means DCA buy check won't fire (negative drop), so only TP triggers.
- **Scope**: Only affects paper bots (V14 Paper + PM Paper). Live Aster unaffected — exchange-side limit orders fill independently of engine's internal TP check.
- **All 3 bots restarted** with fix applied. ZRO/PENDLE should close on 19:00 tick.
