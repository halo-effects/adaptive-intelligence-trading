## False TP Bug Discovery & Fix
- Brett questioned TAO paper bot TP fill — position was deep underwater but recorded 1.48% TP return
- Root cause: `_run_daily_tick()` in `v14_lifecycle_engine.py` passed previous day's aggregate daily high to `_long_dca_tick()`, which compared it against the CURRENT TP target (lowered by intraday DCA layers that didn't exist when the high occurred)
- Also had "Live TP catch-up" block that was redundant with hourly ticks and had same stale-high problem
- Full replay found **106 of 467 TP deals (22.7%) were false TPs** — $9,423 phantom PnL across all coins
- GRASS worst affected (candle data gaps + false TPs), TAO, FET, HYPE, JTO, RENDER, NEAR, ZRO all hit
- Fix applied: daily tick now only handles signals/phase transitions; hourly DCA/TP tick runs on EVERY candle including daily boundary
- Live bot unaffected — uses real exchange limit orders
- Paper bot restarted (PID 2740)
- Key clarification from Brett: NO signal-based exits. Design = TP only (1.48%) or manual CLOSE. No forced selling at 1x leverage.
- Brett took it well but noted concern about 41 days of paper trading not catching this earlier

## Inverted Martingale & Capital Accounting Fixes (Apr 10-11)
- **30% cap removed**: Paper bot had `min(order, capital * 0.3)` that inverted Martingale — ZRO L12 was $50 vs L1 $2,521. Removed from both long and short DCA paths.
- **GAP-13 capital overspend fixed**: Live bot's GAP-13 reset set engine capital to full allocation after each buy, letting L1+L2+L3 total 142% of allocation. Fixed to `allocated_capital - long_cost`.
- **Hard order block → soft cap**: `order > capital: return` changed to `order = min(order, capital)`. L4 now deploys the remainder instead of refusing.
- **Scanner parameter corrections**: BO_PCT 0.40→0.30, taker fee 0.025%→0.035%, SO sizing formula aligned with engine.
- **Grid is effectively 4 layers**: With honest Martingale math, capital depletes by L4. 12-layer config was never reachable without the inverted 30% cap.
- **Velocity-optimized DCA validated**: 365-day backtest on $50K/5 coins: 81.4% annual ROI (long-only), 702 deals, 69% L1, zero pool denials. Fixed strategy beats old inverted (67.2%) and broken GAP-13 (was spending phantom capital).
- **81% is the floor**: Long-only. With bidirectional regime changes (shorts during bear), returns would be significantly higher since stuck capital periods become active short DCA cycles.
