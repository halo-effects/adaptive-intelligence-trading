## Bug: False "Bot Frozen" Alerts — PowerShell Timezone Parsing

### Root Cause
PowerShell's `[datetime]::Parse()` converts ISO timestamps with `+00:00` to local time (Kind=Local). Subtracting `[datetime]::UtcNow` from the local-time result creates a phantom 7-hour offset (PDT = UTC-7). The bot was never frozen — staleness was always ~42 seconds, not ~25,200 seconds.

### Impact
- 6+ unnecessary restarts in 24 hours
- No financial impact (exchange-as-truth preserved all positions)
- All upgrades unaffected

### Fix
Use `[datetimeoffset]::Parse($ts).UtcDateTime` instead of `[datetime]::Parse($ts)`.
