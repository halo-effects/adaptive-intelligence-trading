- Brett's thesis: bear market until Bitcoin halving (~2027), want coins with cyclical volatility that V14 DCA grid can exploit
- **Insight**: What matters isn't raw volatility — it's how often a coin dips and bounces back to complete a DCA cycle
- **Metric**: deals_completed per week, penalized by drawdown and capital lock-up

### Composite Score Formula
```
DCA Score = Realized_PnL × (1 - MaxDD%) × Capital_Freedom / 100
Capital_Freedom = 1 - (open_layers / 24)
```
Rewards: high realized PnL, low drawdown, low capital lock-up (fewer trapped layers)

- **Don't extrapolate narrative from user prompts**: Brett asked about bear market coins. I built a "DeFi revenue" thesis from his initial framing without data. He asked "Did you find that in your research or just from what I originally said?" — busted. Always let data lead.
