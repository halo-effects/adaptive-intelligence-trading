The Four Layers

Each layer has a clear responsibility, defined inputs, and measurable outputs.

ROUTER — Intelligent Phase Management

The brain that decides when to trade and in which direction. Monitors market structure across multiple timeframes, detecting cycle tops via price-momentum divergence analysis and cycle bottoms via a multi-signal conviction stack.

Flips between LONG and SHORT phases — one direction at a time, full capital committed. Minimum phase hold prevents whipsawing. A death cross confirmation gate filters false tops before any transition executes.

“The router doesn’t predict — it confirms. Every transition requires multiple independent signals agreeing.”

DCA Grid — Trading Strategy

Executes grid trading in whichever direction the Router dictates. Layered safety orders buy deeper dips with fixed take-profit cycling. Each completed deal’s profit compounds into the next cycle — returns accelerate over time.

The built-in Coin Scanner scores the entire market daily on five factors: cycling rate, short dominance, drawdown resilience, phase count, and history depth. Coins are graded A+ through F — only qualified coins are tradeable. Equal-weight portfolio across selected coins.

“The engine doesn’t care which direction the market moves — it profits from volatility in either direction.”

Capital Management

Three risk profiles — Low, Medium, High — control leverage, grid density, and position sizing. Conservative leverage with wide safety margins means 65%+ distance to liquidation is typical across all profiles.

Realistic fee modeling is deducted from every trade. All capital deploys in one direction at a time — no hedged positions. Graceful phase transitions: early signals begin unwinding positions while confirmation triggers the full directional flip.

“Your funds never leave your exchange account. The engine manages position sizing — you control the risk level.”

Portfolio Management

Equal-weight allocation across 4–8 coins. The daily scanner refresh re-scores the entire coin universe, and coins must pass extensive history requirements for signal warmup before they’re eligible.

Per-coin sentiment tracking informs the Router’s decisions. An incident detection system flags losing trades for review. The live dashboard shows real-time equity, phase status, trade history, and AI signal state.

“Set your risk profile and let the engine manage everything — coin selection, timing, direction, position sizing, and profit-taking.”
